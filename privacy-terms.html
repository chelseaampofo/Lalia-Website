Your trust matters to us.

This page explains what information we collect, how we look after it, and the terms that apply when you use Lalia. Last updated: September 2026.

Privacy Policy

Lalia ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our website or book a lesson with us, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it.

Information we collect

When you book a free consultation, enquire about a course, or contact us, we collect information such as your name, email address, phone number (if provided), and any details you share with us about your learning goals or preferences so we can personalise your lessons.

Once online payments are introduced, we will also collect billing information needed to process a booking. Payments are handled by a third-party payment provider. We do not store your full card details on our own systems.

If you contact us through the website, we'll keep a record of that conversation so we can respond and follow up.

How we use your information

We use your information to arrange and deliver lessons, communicate with you about bookings, tailor lessons to how you learn best, and respond to enquiries. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Who we share it with

We share relevant details with the tutor delivering your lessons, so they can prepare and personalise your sessions. Once payments go live, necessary billing details will be shared with our payment provider to process transactions securely. We don't share your information with anyone else unless we're required to by law.

How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as you're an active learner with us, and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you return or for record-keeping purposes. We keep your information for as long as you're an active learner with us, and for a period of up to 6 years afterwards for standard business record-keeping purposes.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to ask us what personal information we hold about you, to correct anything that's inaccurate, to ask us to delete it, and to withdraw consent at any time. To do any of this, just get in touch via our Contact Us page.

Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies to help it function properly and to understand how visitors use the site. This includes functional cookies utilized by our integrated platforms, Formspree and Calendly, which are necessary to process your contact messages and seamlessly manage your consultation calendar bookings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, particularly as we introduce new features like online payments. Any changes will be posted on this page.
